## Campaign Blurt

Eons ago, the world fractured.

The temples have one story. The mad have another. What everybody agrees on is this: for one single, unrecorded moment, the sky came apart at the seams, the ground screamed, and something enormous, something that used to watch over all of you, caught the land in its two hands before it could fall apart entirely. The figure did not move again. Dust settled into the folds of it, and the years pressed sand into its shape, until what remained was a still and silent guardian, holding the fracture shut, worshipped by most and feared by the rest.

That was the **Sundering**. 

For a century, people rejoiced at peace, at prosperity, at power, at love, at the presence of devotion. The Sundering bonded people but it left something else broken. Something that surfaced after the 101st Year, haunting generations ever since.

Every so often, when nobody can see the threads of fate, the world holds its breath for what the Vigil folk call the **Draw**. A name appears somewhere it shouldn't: burned into a door, floating in a well's reflection, whispered by a sleeping child in a voice that isn't theirs. Sometimes it's a name. Sometimes just a number, or a birthmark, or the exact colour of someone's eyes. Whoever matches it disappears from their homes, without ever getting a chance to bid farewell to their closest companions. As the rest breathe a sigh of relief, the journey has just begun for the **Called**.

The Called are deprived of a chance to reject fate. The Hollowed Court is where their judgement is passed, where their actions are weighed and their choices dictate whether they live out the fate written for them or whether they play along to the hand that is dealt. Those who don't come back are simply, quietly, removed from the town ledger, and life goes on, because it always has.

There's a saying in the towns,

*Round and round the long hand goes,*
*Writing fate where no one knows.*
*Was it their sins or was it our fear?*
*To defy a call, the price is dear.*

That’s when your name came up, to live your fate out or to defy it as one of the Called.



## House Ruling
- Flanking is +2 to hit
- Better healing
- Help action if proficient and makes sense
- Better Dazed rules
  
Dazed:  
A dazed creature can only do one of the following things on their turn: move, use an action, or use a bonus action. Reactions are unavailable to them. If a creature becomes dazed during their turn and they have already used an action, BA or movement, their turn ends.


- **Klara**: Death House's housemaid, had an affair with Mr. Durst (Gustav)

- **Rose (Rosavalda) and Thorn (Thornboldt) Durst:** The young children of the house. Outside, they appear as frightened, flesh-and-blood kids asking for help, but they are actually harmless magical illusions left behind by the house; their actual skeletal remains lie in the attic where they starved.

- **Gustav and Elisabeth Durst:** The parents and leaders of the dark cult housed in the basement. Elisabeth led the cult to worship Strahd in exchange for eternal youth, while Gustav grew sullen and eventually took his own life after discovering her horrific acts. Both can manifest as dangerous undead ghasts or specters in the lower levels.

- **The Nursemaid (Klara):** The family's nursemaid and Gustav's lover. Murdered by a jealous Elisabeth, her restless, weeping spirit haunts the upper nursery and attic areas.

- **Baby Walter:** The infant son of Gustav and the nursemaid, Klara. Sacrificed by the cult before the adventure begins, his spirit and the energy of other sacrifices fuel the flesh mound monster in the basement. 
Other Entities

- **Lorgoth the Decayer (The Shambling Mound):** A grotesque amalgamation of plant life, flesh, and bone animated by the dark ritual in the dungeon.

- **Sarnax:** A homunculus or pet guardian creature found in certain expansions or interpretations of the library/secret rooms.

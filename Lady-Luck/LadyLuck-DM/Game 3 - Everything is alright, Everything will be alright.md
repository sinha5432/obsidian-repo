City: Uluvin



The Village has been taken over by a cult. They claim that they protect the Village folks, and costs them just a bit of fee

In return, they protect people from "Unsaid horrors" which made folks go mad
In return they just pay them a bit of coin.....just one gold per person per day, and a bit of their blood.


Its alright, a small sacrifice to make for greater good

The town is under the control of clergy now as the citizens feel happy, no need for adventurers, and they would like to shoo them away


The Players to constantly feel similar aura they felt before coming from down, same as before, more than before But they also feel that this aura, seems to be not affecting the Villagers. They seem find, happy...sometimes, overly happy, but all of them have this bandage on their right hand. All of them. 

They also seem weak. Like, physically weak.


If they stay long enough, players will notice these clergymen coming out of the cave where they closed the path to Underdark
